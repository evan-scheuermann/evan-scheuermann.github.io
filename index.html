About Me

I'm a mathematics major with a minor in economics at St. John Fisher University, focusing on applied quantitative methods across machine learning, econometrics, dynamical systems, and statistical modeling.

This summer I'll be joining Rich Products Corporation as a Data & AI Co-op, bringing classroom techniques into a production environment. I'm particularly interested in how rigorous statistical methods translate into decisions that actually matter to a business.

I work primarily in R and Python, with hands-on experience building end-to-end ML pipelines using caret, tidymodels, and ggplot2. Outside of data work, I tutor mathematics at St. John Fisher and have a background in supervisory roles in operations management.

Econometrics R · ggplot2 Fixed Effects ECON 314 In Progress

Steam Power & Manufacturing Productivity, 1850

Log-linear regression with state and industry fixed effects applied to 124,860 firms from the 1850 Census of Manufactures. Quantified the productivity premium of steam adoption and its heterogeneous effects across industries. Produced a choropleth heatmap of state fixed-effect coefficients revealing the outsized economic impact of the California Gold Rush.

+45.2% Steam Premium
51.6% R²
124,860 Observations
