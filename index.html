Three-person project predicting which of 10,000 bank customers will churn. I led algorithm selection and built the random forest and the stacked ensemble. Ames Housing Price Prediction

Individual project comparing Elastic Net and random forest regression on the Ames housing dataset (about 2,930 homes, 82 variables). The random forest won, but only narrowly, which makes its much longer training time hard to justify. The models also ranked features very differently: Elastic Net spread weight across many, while the random forest leaned heavily on overall quality.

  • 88.5%random forest R²
  • 86.2%Elastic Net R²
  • ~15%typical price error

Econometrics R, ggplot2 ECON 314

Steam Power & Manufacturing Productivity, 1850

Three-person project using log-linear regression with state and industry fixed effects on 124,860 firms from the 1850 Census of Manufactures. I designed the research question, built the regression models, and made the choropleth and industry charts. Steam-powered firms produced substantially more, with the largest effects in textiles, construction, and iron and steel. West Coast states stand out on the map, likely because of the Gold Rush.

  • 0.452steam coefficient (log output)
  • 0.516R²
  • 124,860firms
